# Maa Collection — user guide

For everyone who uses the app in the shop. Inside the app: tap **Guide** at the top of any screen (`/guide`: short lessons with pictures, tick each one when learned), or **Help & problems** (`/help`) for quick fixes.

Live app: https://maa-collection-pi.vercel.app

---

## 1. New here? (2 minutes)

1. Open the app on your phone, tap **your name**, type your **PIN** (the owner gives it to you). The phone remembers who logged in last, so next time your name is already picked. Dots show how many digits are in; **Log in** lights up once your name and at least 4 digits are there. A wrong PIN shakes the dots and clears them. With more than 6 people a **Find your name** box appears.
2. Put it on your home screen: Chrome → ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* (or *Install app*); iPhone → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. It gets the green **MC** icon and opens full-screen, straight to your screen while you're logged in.
3. The tabs at the bottom:
   - **Sell**: scan and sell
   - **Exchange**: swap an item from an earlier sale
   - **My sales**: your totals for today and this month
   - **Catalog**: only if the owner gave you catalog or stock work
4. To scan: tap **Scan with camera**; the camera fills the whole screen. Allow camera access, then hold the barcode or QR code inside the box 15–20 cm away; the box flashes green when it reads. **Close** (top right) or Escape returns. A USB/Bluetooth scanner works without tapping anything.
5. **Log out** at the end of the day (⋯ at the top right → **Log out**), especially on a shared phone.

**The top of every screen** shows **Maa Collection** with your name, and two round buttons: **अ / A** (language) and **⋯** (menu). The ⋯ menu holds the colours and Day / Night, **Sound and vibration**, **Guide** and **Log out**.

## 1a. Hindi or English, and scan sounds

- Tap the round **अ** symbol (it shows **A** while you read Hindi) at the top of any screen, or on the login page. The screen changes at once. Your choice is saved **for you**: it follows you to any phone you log in on. Product names, sizes and prices stay as they were entered.
- Hindi covers every screen (selling, owner, catalog, Guide). Printed tags stay in English.
- A good scan shows a **green ✓** with a short **"ting"** (and a small vibration). A failed scan **shakes** with a low buzz. After a sale or exchange a **"Sale done ✓"** card shows the amount with a chime; it closes by itself, or tap it.
- **⋯ → Sound and vibration** turns the sounds and vibration off or on for that phone (Off / On shown on the right).

## 1b. Colours and day/night

- Tap **⋯** at the top right (on the login page: the round ☀ / ☾ symbol). Pick a colour: **Hara** (the default since 2026-11-22: the owner's look — a white page and header, one fresh green, light-green edged cards and boxes, rounded-square green buttons and a green square ← back button before each page's green title), **Bahi** (a clean billing-app look in indigo and orange), **Peacock** (teal), **Marigold** (saffron), **Rani** (pink), **Neel** (indigo), **Mehendi** (henna green), **Sindoor** (vermilion red), **Jamun** (plum purple) or **Aasmani** (sky blue). Then **Day**, **Night** (dark, easier on the eyes in the evening) or **Same as phone**.
- It changes at once and is saved **on that phone** (each phone can have its own look). When Hara came, every phone switched to it once; pick another colour again if you like. Printed tags never change colour.
- Login: tap your **name circle**, then your PIN on the big number keys (⌫ deletes the last digit), then **Log in**.
- The round **Sell** button in the middle of the bottom bar opens the counter from anywhere.

## 1c. Listen to the Guide, and practise

- Open **Guide** (top of the screen, or "New here?" on the login page). Pick your part — **Everyone**, **Selling**, **Stock & tags** or **Owner** — and tap a lesson.
- The 22 lessons follow today's app (updated 2026-11-23): **Selling** has *Many items, one bill*; **Stock & tags** has *New item* (collection → what it is → the four boxes with % → *Sizes & more* → *Save & print tags*) and *The item page*; **Owner** starts with *Companies*, *Company bills* and *Collections and categories*, and *Prices* shows ✎ Edit details.
- A lesson plays like a story: one step at a time, a moving picture of the phone, a few words, and the phone's voice in your language (Hindi or English). It moves on by itself; or tap **Next** / **Back**, or swipe. The speaker button turns the voice off.
- At the end: **Got it ✓** ticks the lesson. In **Sell an item** you can also try a **practice sale** — nothing is saved.
- Hearing nothing? Turn the volume up. For Hindi, the phone needs its Hindi voice: Settings → Text-to-speech → Google → install Hindi (most Android phones have it already).

## 1d. Typing in Hindi or English, and by voice

Every box for words (product name, brand, company name, notes, category names…) has three small buttons:

- **अ / A** — which script this phone types in. In Hindi, type a word in English letters and press space: it turns into Hindi (chikan kurta → चिकन कुर्ता). Your phone's Hindi keyboard works as well. The choice stays for every box on this phone.
- **⇄** — change what is already in the box to the other script (Hindi ↔ English letters).
- **Microphone** — tap, say the words (in the language the box is set to), and they appear in the box after what is there. If the phone asks, allow the microphone for this site. Not every browser can listen; the button hides when it can't.

Spellings made from English letters are phonetic; for an exact Hindi spelling, speak it or use the Hindi keyboard.

**Long forms start fresh — or ask.** New item, the old form and Tag old stock clear themselves when you come back to them later, so old half-typed details never linger. If you had already filled in three or more steps (name, price, sizes), the form asks instead: **Continue where I left off** or **Start a fresh collection / item**. **↺ Start again** at the top clears the form right away (it asks once if something is typed).

**The Done ✓ card.** Whenever something is saved — a sale, a company, a category, new items, a tagged item, a payment — a card pops up with a green tick and the details (for a sale: pieces, price, who gets the credit, whether it is approved or waiting for the owner, stock left). If something could **not** be saved, the card is red with a cross and the reason. It closes by itself, or tap anywhere.

## 2. Who can do what

| Person | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner (admin)** | Everything: dashboard, staff & permissions, settings, reports, all catalog and stock work |
| **Salesman** | Sell (also on behalf of a colleague), exchanges, own sales |
| **Salesman + Catalog** (catalog / QC person) | Add and edit products, photos, names, sizes, colours, categories, companies; tag old stock; generate barcodes; print labels |
| **+ Prices** | Change MRP and selling prices (on an item's page: **✎ Edit details**) |
| **+ Stock** | Receive stock: deliveries, restocks, counts |
| **+ Purchase price** | See and enter the purchase (code) price. Trusted staff only |

The owner sets these in **Menu → Staff & permissions** → tap *Permissions* under a person → tick → **Save permissions**.

## 3. Selling

1. **Sell** → scan the tag (or type the number under the barcode).
2. Check the **photo** and size match the item.
3. **Sell this item** → quantity → agreed price (up to MRP). Selling for a colleague? Pick them in **Credit sale to**.
4. **Record sale**. Stock goes down on every phone at once. Your sales **wait for the owner's check** (a "Waiting" mark in My sales) and count in your totals once he approves them; the owner's own sales count at once.

**Several items for one customer (a bill):**
1. Scan the first item → **Add more items**. It goes onto the bill and, if you used the camera, the camera opens again.
2. Scan each next tag. Scanning the same tag again adds **one more piece**. With the camera, it stays on: the bottom shows the item just added; tap **Done** when all are in.
3. Choose when to type prices: **Price as I scan** (a price box under the camera for each item) or **Price at the end**.
4. The **bill** is one table: photo, item (size, − / + for pieces), **MRP** and the **selling price**. **Fill prices one by one** jumps to the first empty price; Enter moves to the next. The **total** appears as soon as every price is in.
5. Pick **Credit sale to** once for the whole bill → **Save bill · ₹total**. Each item is saved as its own sale (stock, approvals and no-internet saving work as for one item). An item with a problem (price above MRP, not enough stock) stays on the bill in red with the reason. **Clear bill** drops everything not saved.

**A box tag** (*Box of 12* shows on the item): **Sell this item** → **Pieces** to sell 1, 2 … pieces at the piece price, or **Whole boxes** to sell boxes at the **box price** (filled with the box MRP; it cannot be higher). Stock goes down by the pieces either way. On a several-item bill, each box item has a small **pcs / box** switch; with *box*, − / + count boxes and the price is per box.

**Tag won't scan?** (torn tag, broken scanner, dead camera) Tap **Tag won't scan? Enter by hand** under the scan box: type the **item name** as you call it (e.g. "blue kurti"), a **size** if you know it, quantity and the **price the customer paid** → **Record sale**. No stock changes (the app does not know which piece it was) and there is no MRP limit; the owner sees it marked **By hand** and checks it.

**Exchange:** **Exchange** → scan the returned item → choose the sale → scan the new item → check *Customer pays* / *Give back* → **Confirm exchange**.

## 3a. No internet at the counter (plan v18)

Selling never stops when the internet goes.

| What happens | What you do |
|---|---|
| An **amber strip** under the shop's name: *No internet · the counter still works* | Keep selling as usual. |
| Scan a tag (scanner, camera or its QR code) | The item comes from **this phone's copy** (an amber note says so; the stock shown may be old). Tap **Sell this item**, enter the price, **Record sale**. |
| The card says **Saved on this phone** | The sale is kept on this phone. Nothing else to do. |
| Tag won't scan | **Enter by hand** works the same way. |
| See what is waiting | Tap the strip: **Saved on this phone** lists each sale and its time. |
| Internet comes back | The sales are sent **by themselves**; a green message says how many reached the shop. Or tap **Send now**. |
| The app was closed | Open it again: the counter opens even without internet (after it was opened once with internet on this phone). |

Good to know:
- **Use the same phone and stay logged in.** Saved sales are sent only by the person who made them; if you log out, they wait for your next login (the app warns you).
- **Exchange needs internet.**
- **Owner:** these sales arrive in **Approvals** like any other, with a blue **Made offline** mark and the time they were made. They are recorded even if the stock was already 0 — the count can show below zero (for example −1): recount that item and correct it.

## 4. Catalog & old stock (catalog / QC person)

### Tag one old item (~30 seconds)

1. **Catalog → Tag old stock**.
2. Optional: **Scan the tag's barcode** if the item already has one from the company. The app uses it (no new sticker), or tells you the item is already in the app.
3. **📷 Take photo**: 1–4 photos, good light, plain background, the whole item.
4. **Category**, **name** (e.g. "Phulkari Dupatta"), optionally who it is for (**For**: Girls / Boys / Both, **Age**: Adult / Child), tap a **size**. Company is optional. (No colour here: colour is shown and changed only on the product page.)
5. **MRP**, **selling price** (empty = MRP), **pieces counted**.
6. **Save and get barcode**. The label appears.
7. **Next item →**. Category, company, size and For / Age stay filled in.
   Typed something wrong? Tap **✎ Edit this item** before *Next item*: the form comes back filled in; fix it and tap **Save changes**. Same item and barcode, so a printed tag stays valid (reprint it if the name, size, colour or MRP changed). Pieces can't be lowered here; more pieces can be added from **Open item**.
8. At the end: **Print today's labels**. You get one label per piece; stick one on each piece. Choose **A4 sheet** or **Sticker printer 50×25 mm**; stickers print one per label and scan reliably at 203 dpi.

**Needs attention** (Catalog menu) is the catalog to-do list: items without a photo, company or stock (and without a purchase price, for those allowed). Tap an item to fix it.

### New stock from a company (Home → Add new stock)

**Home → Add new stock** (the green tile with a shirt) opens **New item** straight away (owner, 2026-11-22). **Add stock** (the Stock tab) still has the big **New item** button and, under **Other ways**: *Old form* (the earlier New item screen: sizes grid, prices by size, photos), *More pieces of an existing item* and *Tag old stock*.

**New item** (plan v19) asks one thing at a time — there is no *One item / Many items* choice any more: every item can have many sizes. The top is one short row (← · New item · step n of 4 · Start again), a thin bar of the steps, and the step's title with your choices beside it. The ← arrow, and the **phone's back button**, go back **one step at a time** without losing anything (back from the first step leaves New item — to Home for the owner); the choice chips jump straight to a step:

1. **Which collection?** — **Men** and **Women** always come first and always stay (a small 🔒: they cannot be deleted, renamed or hidden), then your other collections. **+ Add** makes a new one on the spot; you stay on this step and the new one shows chosen, glowing, marked **New** — tap it to go on. The **owner** sees **✎** and **🗑** on every other collection: ✎ changes its name and picture; 🗑 asks first and deletes it with its empty categories — a collection that still has items is not deleted (the sheet says how many; delete or move them first). If Women (or Men) was deleted before, its tile is still there: tapping it brings it back.
2. **What is it?** — that section's categories (Jeans 28–36, Shirt M–XXL, Kurta …). **+ Add** makes a new category: type its name and **tap its sizes** — one (S), a few, or a quick range (**S–M**, **S–XL**, 28–36 …); letter, waist and kids' age sizes are listed, others can be typed. You stay on this step with the new category chosen and marked **New**; tap it to go on.
3. **Details & sizes** — the top row is small (← · New item · step 3 of 4 · Start again) so the boxes get the screen:
   - **Four big boxes**: **Company** (*Optional*; or *+ New company…*), **Product name** (left empty, the item is named after its category and number, e.g. *Pajama 013*, the same number as its item code MAA-PJ-013), **Purchase price** (only with the *Purchase price* right) and **Sale price** — the ₹ sign is inside the price boxes.
   - **%** next to *Sale price* opens a small calculator: tap **20 % / 30 % / 50 % / 100 %** or type your own %, it shows *₹700.00 + 50% = ₹1,050.00*; **Use it** puts that price in the box and closes. Leave the sale price empty and it is purchase + the last % anyway (shown in grey in the box).
   - **Sizes & more** (closed until you tap it; its line shows the sizes and pieces, e.g. *No size · 1 pc*): **Which sizes?** — small size buttons from the category's sizes; tap the ones that came. Each chosen size gets a **− pieces +** row just below: 2 × M, 2 × L, 5 × XL. **All sizes** takes every size; **+ Add size** adds one the category lacks and keeps it for next time. Then *Comes as* (pieces or a box), counter price if lower, bill no., stock date, brand.
   Under it a line says what is missing, or counts sizes and pieces, next to **Next**.

   **Company box**: tap it and the list opens just under it (about half the screen at most, scrolls inside; a search line when there are many companies). *Previous companies* are listed under their own heading; **+ New company…** is last. The same box is used on the old form, Tag old stock and Add product.
4. **Check and save** — name, section › category, company, size × pieces, total pieces and items, MRP, purchase per piece and total. Three ways to finish, each saving one item per size (its own barcode each):
   - **Save & print tags** opens the tags page. There, **+ Add next item** starts the next product of the same bill (company, section, bill no., stock date kept) at *What is it?*.
   - **Save & add next** shows *Saved <name>: first – last item code · pieces* and goes straight to *What is it?* of the same bill — the fastest way through a bill.
   - **Save** shows the same message and returns to *Which collection?* with everything cleared — done with this bill.

**Colour appears only on the product page** (owner, 2026-11-22): no screen asks for it or shows it — not the counter, bills, tags, lists or messages. Add or change an item's colour on its product page (*Edit*, *Colours* tab).

**Items that come in a box** (e.g. boys' sandos, 10 or 12 a box): under **Sizes & more** set **Comes as → Box**, tap **6 / 10 / 12** (or type the number), type the **MRP of one piece** and, if the box sells for less, the **Whole box MRP** (empty = MRP × pieces). For the purchase price choose **Per piece** or **Whole box** — with *Whole box* the app shows the price of one piece (₹500 ÷ 12 = ₹41.67). Under the sizes, count **boxes** (3 boxes of 12 = 36 pieces in stock). **One tag is printed per box**; it says *BOX OF 12* and the box price.

**Prices in New item:** type the **Purchase price** first. The **Sale price** next to it can stay empty: it becomes purchase **+ 50%** (₹700 → ₹1,050), shown in grey in the box. Change the % with the **%** button beside it (e.g. 40) — the phone remembers it for the next items. Typing a sale price yourself always wins.

**No size?** In New item's *Sizes & more* you can leave every size untapped: a **No size? Just the pieces:** box appears — set the pieces (− / +, or type) and tap Next. It is saved as one item with no size.

### Companies and Collections (owner, 2026-11-22)

- The app says **Company** everywhere (never *supplier*). A company is **optional** on an item: leave the company box empty, or pick **No company** to clear it.
- **Delete a company** (Catalog right): on its page tap **Delete this company** at the bottom (or Companies → Select → Delete). It asks first. Its items stay, just with no company. **Bills are never changed**: a bill keeps the company's name (shown *(deleted)*), its amounts and payments; edit the bill yourself if you want to change it. Deleting items does not change bills either.
- **Collections** are the groups every item must belong to (New item's first step, *Which collection?*). **Men and Women are fixed** (🔒, owner, 2026-11-22): always listed, never deleted, renamed or hidden — only their Hindi name and picture can change (Categories). **Home → Collections** is one line (collections · categories · products) that opens **Categories** (below). The other collections are renamed (✎) and deleted (🗑, only with **no items**, asks first; its empty categories go too) on the collection tiles in New item. In Bills, what used to be called *collections* (what came in on one New item save) is now called **stock entries**.

### The item page (Products → tap a product; since 2026-11-22)

A short page to read at a glance and act on:
- **Top:** ← Products and **🧾 Make bill** (opens New bill with this product's sizes already on it — see Bills). Then the **photo box**, the name and coloured labels: **company** (orange; tap it to open the company), category, For / Age.
- **Photo** (since 2026-11-23): a product has many sizes but **one photo**. No photo yet → the box says **Add photo**; tap it → **📷 Take photo** or **🖼 Gallery**. With a photo, tap it (the small ✎) → the big photo, a new **📷 / 🖼** photo replaces it on every size, or **Remove photo** (it asks once more: *Remove the photo from all n sizes?*). A size added later with **+ Add size** shows the same photo. Needs the Catalog right; others can tap to see it big.
- **Three figures:** **In stock** (pieces), **Sold** (approved sales), **Stock value** (pieces × purchase price; people without the purchase-price right see the **Last sale** day instead). Then **MRP · Selling · Purchase** (a range when sizes differ).
- **Sizes & pieces:** each size (and colour) with its pieces and *sold n*.
  - **+ Add** — new pieces came in: *How many came in?* **− n +** → **Add n pieces**. It is a stock entry at the item's purchase price, under its company.
  - **Tap a size** — change its **Size** / **Colour** (e.g. give a no-size item size M) or correct **Pieces now** after counting the shelf (saved as a count correction with your name, never overwritten).
  - **+ Add size** — a new size and its pieces (same name, prices, company and colour).
- **✎ Edit details:** name (Hindi or English), company (N/A = none), category, MRP / Selling / Purchase for every size — a box marked **By size** left empty keeps each size's own price.
- **Print label**, **Hide** / **Bring back**, **More** (For / Age), **Delete this item** (owner).
- You only get the buttons your rights allow (sizes and names: Catalog; prices: Prices; purchase price: the purchase-price right; pieces: Stock).

**Bills keep their own copy:** what was on a bill (items, sizes, quantities, rates, amounts, company names) is saved with the bill. Editing, deleting or renaming items or companies later never changes a bill.

### Deleting items (owner)

**Products → Select** → tick cards (a card stands for all its sizes) → **Delete**, or open an item → **Delete this item** at the bottom (choose *Yes, delete it* for this size, or *Delete all N sizes of this design*). The app asks first; it cannot be undone. The item's tags, photos and stock entries go; **past sales stay** with their name, price and amount (reports and profit do not change; in *by category / company* they count under *Other*). If its delivery is on a bill, the bill amount goes down. An item that was part of an exchange is kept (the message says so). Prefer hiding for items you may bring back: open the item → **✎ Edit** → untick **Active**.

### Selecting many (Products, Approvals, All tags, Companies)

**Coming back starts plain** (owner, 2026-11-23): once you open another screen, the cards you ticked are no longer ticked when you return. In the same way, searches clear (the Companies search and place, the search in a bill's item list — the bill's lines stay), and when you press **Back** to a screen, its filters in the address clear too: All tags' search and filters (an open group stays), the period on Reports, Team and a company's page. A link that opens a filtered list on purpose, like *See this company's tags*, always shows it filtered.

On **Products** the **whole card** opens the product (the round **›** on the right); tap anywhere on it.

- **Start**: tap **Select** above the list — or **press and hold any card** for half a second (the phone gives a small buzz). On **Products** only the owner can select (its one action, Delete, is the owner's).
- **Tick**: tap anywhere on a card; a round tick fills in and the card gets a coloured ring. In the Companies and Products lists the rows slide right and the tick sits beside each row, so names stay readable. A design card stands for all its items.
- **One bar at the bottom** (since 2026-11-22 — nothing at the top, the header stays; the menu bar steps aside meanwhile): how many are chosen, then one row of big buttons: **Select all** (everything on the screen; tap again to clear — a dash means some are chosen) · **Cancel** (stop selecting) · the screen's own actions. An action opens a sheet from the bottom; a green message confirms what was done.
  - **Products** (owner): **Delete** only. Name, prices, company, category, For / Age and hiding are changed one item at a time: open the item → **✎ Edit** (untick **Active** to hide it; a hidden item stays in the Products list with a grey **Hidden** mark, and ✎ Edit → tick **Active** brings it back). Print tags from **All tags** or the item's page.
  - **Approvals**: **Approve n** (green) or **Reject** (red; choose one reason for all).
  - **All tags**: **Print n selected**.
  - **Companies** (anyone with the Catalog right): **Delete** only. It asks first (*Delete 2 companies?* → **Yes, delete** / **Cancel**) and cannot be undone. Any company can be deleted: its items **stay**, with no company; its **bills and payments stay exactly as they were**, still showing its name (marked *(deleted)*). (Earlier rule, replaced: a company that has items, bills or payments is **not** deleted — the message says *Can’t delete … it has n items, n bills, n payments* — because its purchase history depends on it. (To hide a company instead, untick **Active** in its **Edit company details**.)

### The Companies page

Home has one small **Companies** line under Today (how many companies, bills, dues); tap it, or **Companies**, to open the Companies page. There: the **search** box with its **microphone** on one line (name, place, contact or phone; English or Hindi); on a second, small line the **place** filter (all the locations you have typed), **Summary ▸**, **Select** (and *n of N* while filtering) (tap to see companies, bills and open bills, collections and pieces, total spend, dues now), and the companies ten at a time (**Previous / Next**): name, location · contact · phone, *bills · dues* and collections. The latest activity comes first (a new bill, or a company you just added); hidden companies last. **+ Add supplier** (top right) opens the add form.

When you add or edit a company, give **Working since** — the day you started buying from them, even years ago. It is separate from the day the company was added in the app; both show under **Details**. Company name is required; location and the date are optional.

### Bills (Home → Bills)

A **collection** is what you add in New item (one save, e.g. Jeans in 6 sizes) or Receive stock — it is **not** a bill. A **bill** is the company's paper bill, entered here. Home has one **Bills** line (how many, how many open, what is due); tap it.

- **Bills** lists every bill, newest bill date first, ten at a time: date, bill no., company, *n items · n pcs* (or *n stock entries* for older bills), total and **Due ₹…** / **Paid** (a bill with no company shows neither).
- **A bill stands on its own (2026-11-22):** a **company is optional** (one, or none). Its lines are the shop's **products** — never typed amounts of stock entries any more.
- **+ New bill** (or **🧾 Make bill** on a product's page, which starts with that product's sizes and its company). Step 1:
  - **Company (optional)** — choose one, *+ New company…*, or leave **N/A**.
  - **Recently added** products are listed below, newest first, a few at a time — **more load by themselves as you scroll** (or tap **Show more**). Type in the search box to find by name, code or barcode.
  - On a product, tap a **size chip** to put that size on the bill — it starts with **its pieces in stock** and **its purchase price** — or **+ All sizes** for the whole delivery. A chip already on the bill shows ✓ and its quantity. A **box item** is billed by the box (boxes × the box's purchase price).
  - **📷 Scan** opens the camera: every tag read puts one piece on the bill (the same tag again = one more); the item and its **− n +** quantity show right under the picture. A **barcode gun** works in the search box (it ends with Enter); a tag's QR link works too. An unknown code is said.
  - The lines are a **table like the paper bill** (since 2026-11-22): **S.N · Product · Qty · Purchase ₹ · Total ₹**, then the **Total** row (units and ₹). Tap **Qty** or **Purchase ₹** and type what the paper says — the keyboard's **Next** goes to the next box, an empty price is **N/A**. **✕** next to the size takes a line off; **Undo** in its place brings it back for a few seconds. Colour is never shown on a bill.
- **Step 2 — Check with the paper bill**: **Party** (the company or *No company*), **Bill no.** (optional), **Bill date** (can be earlier), then the same table (**S.N · Product · Qty · Purchase ₹ · Total ₹**) with its total; **Note**; **Save bill**. The phone's back button goes back to step 1 with nothing lost.
- **A bill's page**: the total; with a company its amount, **due** and **Mark paid** (the company's page lists the bill and its number opens it); with no company just *No company* — nothing is owed. **As on the paper bill**, then **✎ Edit bill**: the same editor — quantities and rates change, lines go or come, the company changes (not when it has payments). **The bill keeps its own copy**: changing or deleting products later never changes it. **Delete bill** (no payments on it) asks first.
- Bills made earlier from **stock entries** still open and work as before (take a stock entry off, + Add stock entries, Edit bill).
- **Dues** of a company = all its bill amounts − all its payments. A payment can name one of its bills (*For bill no.*), or go to the running account.

### A company's page (Companies → tap a company)

The figures on top, for the period you pick (**All time · This month · Last 30 days · This year · Dates…**): **Bills** (how many are still open and how many paid), **Purchases**, **Dues now** (what you still owe, all time), **Pieces** (and items), **Collections** (designs bought) and the **Last bill**. Purchases and dues show only to people with the *Purchase price* right. Below: **+ New item** and **Receive more**, then tabs:

- **Bills** — every bill (deliveries with the same bill no. are one bill), newest first, **ten at a time** (Previous / Next; the newest 100): date, bill no., items · pieces · purchase, and **Due ₹…** / **Paid**. Open a bill's items, print its tags, or tap **Mark paid** when you have paid it in full. *(est.)* means the purchase price was not recorded on the day of that delivery, so today's price is used.
- **Payments** — the **Record a payment** form, then **Save payment**: amount, day, **for bill no.** (pick the bill; or *Running account* for an advance / a lump sum), note. A bill shows *Paid* once the payments against its number cover it. Dues = all purchases − all payments.
- **Products** — the company's collections with sizes, MRP and pieces in stock; **Company tags**.
- **By type** — what you bought from them by section › category.
- **Details** — contact, phone, **location**, **Working since**, **Added on**, GSTIN, notes; **Edit company details**.

The Companies list shows each company's bills and dues too.

### Other ways to add stock

- **More pieces of an existing item** (needs *Stock*): scan each piece. Scanning the same tag again adds one more.

### Photos while creating tags (optional)

- **New item / Old form**: the main photo (right of the name) goes on every item.
- **Tag old stock**: 1–4 photos, as before.
- If a photo fails (e.g. weak signal), the items are still saved; the labels page says so — add it later on the item's page: tap the photo box.

### Categories: collections and their categories (plan v19; owner, 2026-11-22)

**Home → Collections** (the line under Today, like Companies and Bills: *collections · categories · products*) or **Menu → Categories**. The shop's items are sorted two ways: first the **collection** (**Men, Women** — always there — and any you add, e.g. *Kids*), then **what it is** (a *category*: Jeans, Shirt, Kurti, Saree …). New item asks these two questions first.

- **The tiles on top** are the collections, each with its picture and **how many products** it has. Tap one to see its categories; the first opens by itself. If Women (or Men) was deleted before, its tile is still there: tapping it brings it back.
- **The figures** under the tiles, for the collection you are on: **Categories**, **Products**, **Pieces in stock** and, with the *Purchase price* right, **Stock value** (pieces × purchase price).
- **Each category** shows its products · items · pieces (and what they are worth), its code and its sizes. **✎** opens its form (name, code, sizes, Hindi name, collection); **🗑** (owner) asks first and deletes it — only when it has **no items** (the sheet says how many are left otherwise). **Jeans, Jacket and Shirt** carry a 🔒: they always stay and keep their name (their sizes can still be added in New item with *+ Add size*).
- **+ Add category** adds one under the tile you are on (the **Under** box lets you pick another collection). Give it a **Hindi name** too if you like.
- **+ Add collection** at the bottom: name, Hindi name, picture. Collections are renamed or deleted on New item's *Which collection?* tiles (✎ / 🗑).
- **Needs sorting**: this tile appears only while some categories are under no section yet (your old categories after the update). On each row choose **Move under → Women** (for example) and tap **Move**; the row leaves the list and the tile disappears when everything is sorted. **Until a category is sorted, New item will not accept it** — it says *Sort this category under Men, Women, Boys or Girls first*. Sorting takes a couple of minutes once.

### Sizes of each category

Each category has its sizes, set by the owner in **Categories** (tap a category → *Sizes*, or a preset: S–XXL, M–XXL, Ladies 26–32, Gents 28–36, Free). Starting lists: shirts, T-shirts, dresses, night suits S–XXL; kurtas and kurtis M–XXL; **Ladies Jeans** 26–32 and **Gents Jeans** 28–36 (separate categories); sarees and dupattas Free. When you add stock, **choose the category first** — its sizes are shown first (tap *Pick all* for every size); other sizes are under *More sizes*.

### Tags (labels)

Every piece gets a **hang tag, 40 × 60 mm**: shop name, **QR code**, the **size** big, **every size of its category with a tick box** — this piece's box is filled (e.g. ☐S ■M ☐L ☐XL ☐XXL), so on a printed tag you can tick another size with a pen — the name, **ART.** (item code) with the secret code price on the right if switched on, the **barcode**, and the **MRP**.

- **Products (since 2026-11-22):** one plain list of **every product** — no search, filters or *Show* switch for now. Items with the same **name, company and MRP** are **one small card**: the photo, the name, then three coloured labels — the **company** (orange), **Items: n** and the **pieces** in stock (purple) — the line **pieces × purchase price per piece = total**, e.g. *8 pcs × ₹900.00 = ₹7,200.00* (a range per piece when sizes cost differently; people without the purchase-price right don't see this line), and every size with its pieces. Anything not filled in shows **N/A** (no photo, no company, no size, no purchase price). Tap the name to open the item (its MRP, selling price, sizes and tags are there). Hidden items stay in the list, marked **Hidden**. The line at the top counts designs, items and pieces.
- **All tags** keeps the bigger card: photos, sizes, MRP, one design QR; tap **Show 6 tags** to see them one by one, or pick **Show: Every tag** at the top; **Print tags** on a card prints that design. Each piece still gets its own tag and barcode.
- **All tags** (More → Labels, or *See all tags →* on any print page): every tag made in the app, in groups — by **Date** made (Today, Yesterday, …), **Company**, **Category** or **Delivery** (one bill). Filter by name or code, company (or *No company*), category, who made it, when (Today / 7 days / 30 days / All time) and *Only items in stock*. **Grid** shows each group's tags in lines; **Rows** puts each group on one line you swipe sideways (your phone remembers the choice). **Print this group** prints just that group; **Print these tags** prints everything shown. Tap a tag to open its item. A company's page has *See this company's tags*.
- Print: after saving, or **Labels → print today's labels**. Choose **Tags on A4** (16 per page: cut, punch the circle, thread), **Tag printer 40×60 mm**, or **Sticker printer 50×25 mm** (small barcode sticker, no QR).
- **QR code:** staff point the normal phone camera at it → the item opens (sellers: Sell screen with the item ready; catalog/stock/owner: the product page). You must be logged in on that phone; customers only see the login page. In-app and USB scanners read the QR too.

**Sample tags to practise scanning:** 8 demo tags in `public/sample-scanner-images/` (in the code), also online at https://maa-collection-pi.vercel.app/sample-scanner-images/all-tags.png, …/all-tags-print-a4.pdf, and one per tag (e.g. …/02-anarkali-kurta-M-MAA-KU-001.png). Open one on a computer screen and scan it with **Sell → Scan with camera**.

### Photos of a product

Add the photo while making the item (**New item**, **Tag old stock**), or later on the item page: tap the **photo box** at the top (since 2026-11-23). One photo for the whole product — every size, the Products card and the counter show it. Tap the box again to change it or **Remove photo**.

## 5a. Sales team dashboard (owner)

**Team** in the menu: number of salesmen; **share of sales** per seller (bar, amount, %); **each seller day by day** (small chart per person, same scale — the best day and its amount under each chart; "below MRP" = how much they bargain down on average); and a table with every number. Pick Today / 7 days / 30 days / This month. A sale counts for the person chosen in *Credit sale to*; exchanges are included.

## 5b. Clear stock (owner)

For a fresh count or a new season: **More → Clear stock**. It shows what is in stock now; tap **Clear all stock…**, enter **your PIN** and tap **Set all pieces to 0**.

- Every item's pieces become 0. Items, barcodes, prices, photos and past sales **stay**; each item's history shows "Stock cleared" with your name and the date.
- It cannot be undone. Afterwards, count the shelves and add pieces with **More pieces of an existing item** (scan each piece).
- Only the owner can do it, and a wrong PIN changes nothing.

## 5c. Approvals (owner)

Every sale and exchange a salesman records **waits for you**. Home shows **"n entries waiting"**; tap it (or **More → Approvals**).

- Entries are grouped by day, newest first. Each card shows what was sold, quantity, the price per piece next to the **MRP and usual price** (and "x% below MRP"), who sold and who recorded it. Hand-entered sales carry a **By hand** mark with the name and size the salesman typed.
- **Profit or loss** (owner only): each scanned sale shows its purchase price and a tag — green edge and **▲ Profit ₹…** when it sold above the purchase price, red card and **▼ Loss ₹…** when it sold below it (hand-entered sales: *No purchase price*). Each day shows its profit (or loss), and Home's Approvals tile says **n at a loss** in red. Salesmen never see any of this.
- **Approve** one card, **Approve all for this day**, or **Approve everything**. Approved entries then count in Home, Reports, Sales team, My sales and the CSV.
- **Reject** → choose a reason (Wrong price · Wrong item · Duplicate · Not sold · Other), add a note if you like → **Reject entry**. A scanned sale's pieces go **back into stock** (the item's history shows it); a hand-entered sale returns nothing. A rejected exchange is undone (the new item comes back, the returned piece goes out again).
- A decision is final and made once; a second tap, or another phone, changes nothing. If a sale has an exchange on it, reject the exchange first. Your last 50 decisions are listed below the queue.
- Your own entries are approved as you record them.

## 5. Owner set-up checklist

1. **Companies** (Menu → Companies) and **categories** (Menu → Categories: sort each under **Men / Women / Boys / Girls** — empty the *Needs sorting* tile once — each category has a 2-letter code used in SKUs and its sizes).
2. **Staff & permissions** (More → Staff & permissions): **Add a person** (name, role, PIN); **Set PIN** to change a PIN; tick extra rights per salesman; **Remove** someone who left (asks once — they can't log in any more, their past sales stay) and **Bring back** from *Removed staff* if needed.
3. **Settings → Code price** (optional): a 10-letter word with no repeated letters. Labels then show the purchase price in letters, e.g. BLACKHORSE: ₹450 = **CKE**.
4. Add stock (or let the catalog person tag old stock).
5. Daily: **Home** for today's sales and the entries waiting for you; **Reports** and **Team** for the month, profit and each salesperson; **Products** / **Needs attention** for low and empty stock; **Activity** for every sale and exchange.
6. **Reports** (Menu → Reports): best sellers, items not selling for 30 days, sales by category / company / salesperson for Today, 7 days, 30 days or this month; **download sales and stock as CSV** for your accountant (opens in Excel / Google Sheets).

## 6. Required vs optional

| When | Required | Optional |
|---|---|---|
| Tag old stock | Category, name, MRP, pieces | Photo (recommended), For / Age, size (= Free), company, selling price (= MRP), purchase price |
| New item (steps) | Collection, what it is, the purchase or the sale price | Company, product name (= category + number), sizes (= no size), pieces per size (= 1), counter price (= MRP), bill no., stock date (= today), brand (= none) |
| Old form | Company, product name, category, sale price (MRP), purchase price (with the *Purchase price* right) | Photo, For / Age, pieces (= 1), counter price (= MRP), bill no., sizes (= Free), brand (= company) |
| Sale | Scan, quantity, price | Credit to a colleague |
| Company | Name | Contact, phone, GSTIN, location, working since, notes |

## 7. Tips for moving all old stock into the app

- Work **shelf by shelf**, one category at a time. The app keeps the category for you.
- Count the pieces of one design + size together and tag them as **one item with that count**.
- **Print and stick the same day**, so labels don't get mixed up.
- Put labels in the **same place** on every item.
- Two catalog people can work at once on different shelves.
- Use **one name per design** ("Cotton Straight Kurta"), so its sizes group together.

## 8. Problems & fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Camera doesn't open | Allow the camera for the site in browser settings and reload. Only works on the https:// address |
| Barcode not read | Flat in the green box, 15–20 cm, good light; or type the number under the barcode |
| "No item found" | Not in the app yet: a catalog person tags it (Tag old stock) |
| "Only N left in stock" | Stock in the app is lower than on the shelf; someone with *Stock* receives the missing pieces |
| I can't see Catalog / Prices | Ask the owner to tick that right for you |
| Wrong PIN | Check your name is selected; the owner can set a new PIN |
| Photo won't upload | Better signal; photos are made small automatically. On a product's page a new photo replaces the old one on every size |
| Printing from a phone | Labels page → Print → your printer, or "Save as PDF" |
