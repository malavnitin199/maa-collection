# Maa Collection — user guide

For everyone who uses the app in the shop. Inside the app: tap **Guide** at the top of any screen (`/guide`: short lessons with pictures, tick each one when learned), or **Help & problems** (`/help`) for quick fixes.

Live app: https://maa-collection-pi.vercel.app

---

## 1. New here? (2 minutes)

1. Open the app on your phone, tap **your name**, type your **PIN** (the owner gives it to you).
2. Put it on your home screen: Chrome → ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* (or *Install app*); iPhone → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. It gets the green **MC** icon and opens full-screen, straight to your screen while you're logged in.
3. The tabs at the bottom:
   - **Sell**: scan and sell
   - **Exchange**: swap an item from an earlier sale
   - **My sales**: your totals for today and this month
   - **Catalog**: only if the owner gave you catalog or stock work
4. To scan: **Scan with camera**, allow the camera once, hold the barcode flat in the green box 15–20 cm away. A USB/Bluetooth scanner works without tapping anything.
5. **Log out** at the end of the day, especially on a shared phone.

## 1a. Hindi or English, and scan sounds

- Tap **हिन्दी** (or **English**) at the top of any screen, or on the login page. The screen changes at once. Your choice is saved **for you**: it follows you to any phone you log in on. Product names, sizes and prices stay as they were entered.
- Hindi covers every screen (selling, owner, catalog, Guide). Printed tags stay in English.
- A good scan shows a **green ✓** with a short **"ting"** (and a small vibration). A failed scan **shakes** with a low buzz. After a sale or exchange a **"Sale done ✓"** card shows the amount with a chime; it closes by itself, or tap it.
- **🔊 / 🔇** at the top turns the sounds and vibration off or on for that phone.

## 1b. Colours and day/night

- Tap the **palette** button (top of any screen, also on the login page). Pick a colour: **Peacock** (teal, the default), **Marigold** (saffron), **Rani** (pink), **Neel** (indigo) or **Mehendi** (henna green). Then **Day**, **Night** (dark, easier on the eyes in the evening) or **Same as phone**.
- It changes at once and is saved **on that phone** (each phone can have its own look). Printed tags never change colour.
- Login: tap your **name circle**, then your PIN on the big number keys (⌫ deletes the last digit), then **Log in**.
- The round **Sell** button in the middle of the bottom bar opens the counter from anywhere.

## 2. Who can do what

| Person | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner (admin)** | Everything: dashboard, staff & permissions, settings, reports, all catalog and stock work |
| **Salesman** | Sell (also on behalf of a colleague), exchanges, own sales |
| **Salesman + Catalog** (catalog / QC person) | Add and edit products, photos, names, sizes, colours, categories, companies; tag old stock; generate barcodes; print labels |
| **+ Prices** | Change MRP and selling prices (also "rates for the whole design", by size) |
| **+ Stock** | Receive stock: deliveries, restocks, counts |
| **+ Purchase price** | See and enter the purchase (code) price. Trusted staff only |

The owner sets these in **Menu → Staff & permissions** → tap *Permissions* under a person → tick → **Save permissions**.

## 3. Selling

1. **Sell** → scan the tag (or type the number under the barcode).
2. Check the **photo**, size and colour match the item.
3. **Sell this item** → quantity → agreed price (up to MRP). Selling for a colleague? Pick them in **Credit sale to**.
4. **Record sale**. Stock goes down on every phone at once.

**Exchange:** **Exchange** → scan the returned item → choose the sale → scan the new item → check *Customer pays* / *Give back* → **Confirm exchange**.

## 4. Catalog & old stock (catalog / QC person)

### Tag one old item (~30 seconds)

1. **Catalog → Tag old stock**.
2. Optional: **Scan the tag's barcode** if the item already has one from the company. The app uses it (no new sticker), or tells you the item is already in the app.
3. **📷 Take photo**: 1–4 photos, good light, plain background, the whole item.
4. **Category**, **name** (e.g. "Phulkari Dupatta"), tap a **size**, type the **colour**. Company is optional.
5. **MRP**, **selling price** (empty = MRP), **pieces counted**.
6. **Save and get barcode**. The label appears.
7. **Next item →**. Category, company and size stay filled in.
8. At the end: **Print today's labels**. You get one label per piece; stick one on each piece. Choose **A4 sheet** or **Sticker printer 50×25 mm**; stickers print one per label and scan reliably at 203 dpi.

**Needs attention** (Catalog menu) is the catalog to-do list: items without a photo, company or stock (and without a purchase price, for those allowed). Tap an item to fix it.

### Other ways to add stock (Catalog → Add stock)

- **Many different products** from one company: one card per product with its own prices.
- **One design in sizes × colours**: e.g. a kurta in M/L/XL × 3 colours. Every combination gets a barcode.
  - Enter one MRP, selling and purchase price for all sizes. If a size costs more (e.g. XL), open **Price by size**, and type that size's own MRP / selling / purchase price. Grey numbers are the default; green ones are the size's own. Every colour of that size gets the same price; colour never changes the price.
  - Later: open any of its products → **Rates for the whole design** → change the default or one size → **Apply**. Past sales keep their prices.
- **More of existing items** (needs *Stock*): scan each piece. Scanning the same tag again adds one more.

### Photos while creating tags (optional)

- **New design**: after adding the colours, each colour has **📷 Photo / 🖼 Gallery**. Every size of that colour gets the photo.
- **Quick add**: each product card has **📷 Photo / 🖼 Gallery**.
- **Tag old stock**: 1–4 photos, as before.
- If a photo fails (e.g. weak signal), the items are still saved; the labels page says so — add it later on the item's page → Photos.

### Sizes of each category

Each category has its sizes, set by the owner in **Categories** (tap a category → *Sizes*, or a preset: S–XXL, M–XXL, Ladies 26–32, Gents 28–36, Free). Starting lists: shirts, T-shirts, dresses, night suits S–XXL; kurtas and kurtis M–XXL; **Ladies Jeans** 26–32 and **Gents Jeans** 28–36 (separate categories); sarees and dupattas Free. When you add stock, **choose the category first** — its sizes are shown first (tap *Pick all* for every size); other sizes are under *More sizes*.

### Tags (labels)

Every piece gets a **hang tag, 40 × 60 mm**: shop name, **QR code**, the **size** big with the colour, **every size of its category with a tick box** — this piece's box is filled (e.g. ☐S ■M ☐L ☐XL ☐XXL), so on a printed tag you can tick another size with a pen — the name, **ART.** (item code) with the secret code price on the right if switched on, the **barcode**, and the **MRP**.

- Print: after saving, or **Labels → print today's labels**. Choose **Tags on A4** (16 per page: cut, punch the circle, thread), **Tag printer 40×60 mm**, or **Sticker printer 50×25 mm** (small barcode sticker, no QR).
- **QR code:** staff point the normal phone camera at it → the item opens (sellers: Sell screen with the item ready; catalog/stock/owner: the product page). You must be logged in on that phone; customers only see the login page. In-app and USB scanners read the QR too.

**Sample tags to practise scanning:** 8 demo tags in `public/sample-scanner-images/` (in the code), also online at https://maa-collection-pi.vercel.app/sample-scanner-images/all-tags.png, …/all-tags-print-a4.pdf, and one per tag (e.g. …/02-anarkali-kurta-M-MAA-KU-001.png). Open one on a computer screen and scan it with **Sell → Scan with camera**.

### Photos of an existing product

**Products** → tap the item → **Photos** → *Take photo* / *From gallery*. The first photo is the **main** one, shown at the counter. Use *Make main* to change it, or *Delete*. Up to 8 photos per product; they're made smaller automatically before uploading.

## 5a. Sales team dashboard (owner)

**Team** in the menu (or Home → *Sales team graphs*): number of salesmen; **share of sales** per seller (bar, amount, %); **each seller day by day** (small chart per person, same scale — tap a day for its amount; "below MRP" = how much they bargain down on average); and a table with every number. Pick Today / 7 days / 30 days / This month. A sale counts for the person chosen in *Credit sale to*; exchanges are included.

## 5. Owner set-up checklist

1. **Companies** (Menu → Companies) and **categories** (Menu → Categories, each with a 2-letter code used in SKUs).
2. **Staff & permissions**: add each person with a PIN; tick rights per salesman.
3. **Settings → Code price** (optional): a 10-letter word with no repeated letters. Labels then show the purchase price in letters, e.g. BLACKHORSE: ₹450 = **CKE**.
4. Add stock (or let the catalog person tag old stock).
5. Daily: **Home** for today/month, sales per person, low stock; **Activity** for every sale and exchange.
6. **Reports** (Menu → Reports): best sellers, items not selling for 30 days, sales by category / company / salesperson for Today, 7 days, 30 days or this month; **download sales and stock as CSV** for your accountant (opens in Excel / Google Sheets).

## 6. Required vs optional

| When | Required | Optional |
|---|---|---|
| Tag old stock | Category, name, MRP, pieces | Photo (recommended), size (= Free), colour, company, selling price (= MRP), purchase price |
| Quick add | Company, category, each product's name and MRP | Brand, bill no., selling/purchase price, size, colour |
| Sale | Scan, quantity, price | Credit to a colleague |
| Company | Name | Contact, phone, GSTIN, notes |

## 7. Tips for moving all old stock into the app

- Work **shelf by shelf**, one category at a time. The app keeps the category for you.
- Count the pieces of one design + size together and tag them as **one item with that count**.
- **Print and stick the same day**, so labels don't get mixed up.
- Put labels in the **same place** on every item.
- Two catalog people can work at once on different shelves.
- Use **one name per design** ("Cotton Straight Kurta"), so its sizes and colours group together.

## 8. Problems & fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Camera doesn't open | Allow the camera for the site in browser settings and reload. Only works on the https:// address |
| Barcode not read | Flat in the green box, 15–20 cm, good light; or type the number under the barcode |
| "No item found" | Not in the app yet: a catalog person tags it (Tag old stock) |
| "Only N left in stock" | Stock in the app is lower than on the shelf; someone with *Stock* receives the missing pieces |
| I can't see Catalog / Prices | Ask the owner to tick that right for you |
| Wrong PIN | Check your name is selected; the owner can set a new PIN |
| Photo won't upload | Better signal or a smaller photo; max 8 photos per product |
| Printing from a phone | Labels page → Print → your printer, or "Save as PDF" |
