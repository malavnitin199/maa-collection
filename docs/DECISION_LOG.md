# Architecture & product decision log (living)

Record agreed decisions here. Do not treat provisional ideas as final.

| ID | Date | Status | Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-001 | 2026-11-14 | Proposed | Next.js App Router in one repository | Built-in server layer supports secure business operations; POS remains client-heavy | Foundation |
| ADR-002 | 2026-11-14 | Proposed | PostgreSQL with transactional sales/stock ledger | Atomic checkout, constraints, audits | P2 onward |
| ADR-003 | 2026-11-14 | Proposed | Offline sales remain provisional pending sync | Offline counters cannot know real-time final stock across devices | P6 |
| ADR-004 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | Scan-to-sell without a multi-item cart: each scan deducts stock and records one sale with a per-customer bargained unit price entered by the salesperson, attributed to that salesperson | Owner: every customer negotiates a different price; MRP is only a ceiling | POS flow (P4) stores the entered price and an MRP snapshot per sale line. MRP cap is an engineering assumption pending owner confirmation (relates to D-08). |
| ADR-005 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | One scannable code per item: an EAN-13 barcode (no QR on labels). Generated barcodes use the GS1 in-store prefix `200`; manufacturer EAN-13s are accepted as-is | Matches clothing tags and cheap 1D scanners; in-store prefix can't clash with manufacturer codes | Labels, product creation |
| ADR-006 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | Shared PostgreSQL on Neon (free plan, via Vercel Marketplace, Singapore), Drizzle ORM; PGlite for local dev/tests | Admin-created products and sales must be visible on every device | Replaces the browser-only demo store |
| ADR-007 | 2026-11-15 | Implemented (plan v2) | Staff log in with name + 4–6 digit PIN (scrypt), signed http-only session cookie; roles admin / salesman checked on the server | Fast at the counter on a phone; real accounts needed for "sale on behalf of" and per-person dashboards. Better Auth (blueprint) deferred until email/password or multi-shop accounts are needed | Auth, all server actions |
| ADR-008 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | Salesmen may record a sale or exchange credited to another salesperson; both `sold_by` (credited) and `recorded_by` are stored | Owner request (Vishal, Nitin, Pradeep, Balram) | Sales, exchanges, dashboards |
| ADR-009 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | Owner grants per-person permissions on top of the two roles: `catalog`, `prices`, `stock`, `costs` (stored on `staff.permissions`); admins have all | A salesman can become a catalog / QC or stock person without becoming an admin | Every admin page/action checks a specific right; menus filtered |
| ADR-010 | 2026-11-15 | Implemented | Product photos stored in Postgres (`product_images.data`, bytea) and served by `/api/product-images/<id>`; `url` column ready for Vercel Blob | Works identically in dev, tests and production with no extra service; creating a Blob store was blocked by the assistant's safety check pending the owner's approval | Neon free tier holds roughly 5–7k photos at ~100–250 KB; move to Blob before that |
| ADR-011 | 2026-11-15 | Implemented | Photos are resized on the phone (≤1280 px JPEG) before upload; server sniffs JPEG/PNG/WebP and caps size and count (8) | Phone photos are 3–8 MB; mobile data and request limits | Server action body limit 4 MB |
| ADR-012 | 2026-11-15 | Implemented | Old stock is tagged through the same retry-safe receipt flow as deliveries (supplier optional, brand "Unbranded"); "today's labels" prints everything one person entered today | One code path for all stock arrivals; easy end-of-session printing | `stock_receipts.supplier_id` nullable use |
| ADR-013 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | Prices follow the **size**, never the colour: a design has default MRP / selling / purchase prices and any size may have its own MRP, selling and purchase price, applied to every colour of that size. The product-page "Rates for the whole design" edits by size too | Bigger sizes cost more to buy and carry a higher MRP; entering a price per colour is needless work | No migration (each size × colour is already its own product row). Rule in `src/lib/size-prices.ts`, checked in `createDesign` / `updateDesignRates`; selling ≤ MRP per size. Plan: `docs/PLAN_SIZE_PRICES.md` |
| ADR-014 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat, photo of a fashion hang tag) | Tags are **50 × 80 mm hang tags with a QR code** next to the EAN-13 barcode: shop, QR, size boxed big with the colour, the design's sizes with this one highlighted, name, ART. (= SKU) + code price, barcode, MRP. Replaces the "no QR on labels" part of ADR-005; the EAN-13 stays the item's identity | Owner wants tags like branded stores; staff can open an item with any phone camera | `HangTag`, `QrCode` (`qrcode` package); formats Tags on A4 (9/page), Tag printer 50×80 mm, Sticker 50×25 mm (barcode only). QR content: see D-QR-1. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V4_QR_TAGS_GUIDE.md` |
| ADR-015 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Each category has a size list** (shop order), pre-filled and editable by the owner: shirts/tees/dresses S–XXL, kurtas/kurtis M–XXL, sarees/dupattas Free; **jeans by gender as two categories**, Ladies Jeans 26–32 and Gents Jeans 28–36. Stock entry offers the category's sizes first. Tags shrink to **40 × 60 mm** and print **every size of the category with a tick box** (this piece's filled) so a size can be marked by pen | Prices are often the same across sizes; the owner wants to decide/mark sizes on printed tags. Sizes depend on the category (and gender for jeans) | `categories.sizes text[]` (migration 0005, which also fills defaults for known codes and adds LJ/GJ); `src/lib/size-lists.ts`; `tagSizeRange` / `tagSizesFor`; A4 = 16 tags/page. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V5_CATEGORY_SIZES_SMALL_TAG.md` |
| ADR-016 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Sales team dashboard** `/admin/team` (owner only): number of salesmen, each seller's share of net sales, daily sales per seller on one shared scale, and a full table; net = sales credited (`sold_by`) + exchange differences credited. **Optional photo while creating tags**: one per colour in New design (every size of that colour gets it) and one per product in Quick add. A photo used by several items is stored once: the others' `product_images.url` points to `/api/product-images/<id>`, and deleting the original hands its bytes to a sharer | Owner wants to see each seller's performance and to identify items by photo from the moment their tags are made | `teamReport`, `periodDays`, `ShareBars` / `DailyColumns` (one validated hue, no chart library); `addPhotoToProducts`, `addPhotoToItemsAction`, `PhotoSlot`. No migration. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V6_TEAM_DASHBOARD_PHOTOS.md` |
| ADR-017 | 2026-11-15 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Hindi + English**: each person chooses (saved on `staff.language`, migration 0006; falls back to the phone's last choice, then English); switch with one tap in the header or on the login page, re-rendered in place. Round 1 = salesman screens; owner/catalog screens next. **Payment-app style feedback**: tick + "ting" + vibration on a good scan, shake + buzz on a failed one, "Sale done ✓" card + chime after a sale/exchange; sound on by default with a per-phone mute | Most counter staff read Hindi more easily; a clear, audible confirmation avoids double scans and missed sales in a busy shop | Own small dictionaries (no i18n library), keys on `DomainError` for server messages; Web Audio tones (no audio files); reduced-motion respected. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V7_HINDI_AND_SCAN_FEEDBACK.md`; reference §15–16 |
| ADR-018 | 2026-11-16 | Implemented (owner request, chat) | **Look and feel v8**: five colour themes named after Indian dyes (Peacock default, Marigold, Rani, Neel, Mehendi) and Day / Night / Same as phone, chosen **per phone** (not per person); a shop-made design — Rozha One + Mukta type, a woven zari border, hang-tag shaped main actions, floating dock with a raised Sell button, PIN keypad login, a dyed scan zone | Owner wants the app easier to use and more catchy for retailers; themes also let a future retailer pick a look close to their brand | CSS variables behind Tailwind `brand-*` (no new dependency, no DB change); appearance in `localStorage`, applied before first paint. Printed tags unaffected. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V8_LOOK_AND_FEEL.md`; reference §17 |
| ADR-019 | 2026-11-16 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Edit after tagging** (Tag old stock): fix the item just saved in place — same barcode, SKU and piece count; prices by the *Prices* right or by the item's creator before its first sale. **Guide voice with the phone's own voice** (Web Speech API, en-IN / hi-IN) reading the on-screen lesson text with step highlighting, "Listen to all", tap-to-hear tag parts, and a **practice sale** that saves nothing | Owner wanted an edit option at the end of tagging and a Guide that talks people through the app in their language; the phone's voice needs no files, costs nothing and never goes out of sync with the text | No DB change. Lowering a piece count deferred (D-ST-2). Plan: `docs/PLAN_V9_GUIDE_VOICE_EDIT.md`; reference §18 |
| ADR-020 | 2026-11-16 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **The Guide is short stories**: pick your part → a lesson plays one step at a time with an animated scene, ≤ 7 words on screen and a fuller spoken line; limits are enforced by a test. Replaces the long lesson page and the page-level Listen player of ADR-019 (voice helper, practice sale and ✓ learned stay) | Owner: the Guide was too long for people to take guidance from; they want minimum words, better animations and guidance | Lessons are data in `src/features/guide/stories.ts` (both languages side by side). No DB change. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V10_SHORT_GUIDE.md`; reference §19 |
| ADR-021 | 2026-11-16 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Clear stock** is an owner-only action that sets every item's count to 0 and records a `cleared` stock movement per item; it never deletes items, sales or history, and needs the owner's PIN. The 2026-11-16 removal of test data from the live database was a one-off clean-up (backup taken first), not an app feature | Owner wanted a fresh start and a way to empty stock from the app; a delete-everything button would risk the shop's records once real sales exist | New movement reason `cleared` (text column, no migration); keeps the append-only ledger. Plan: `docs/PLAN_V11_FRESH_START_CLEAR_STOCK.md` |
| ADR-022 | 2026-11-16 | Confirmed by owner (chat) | **Sell without scanning + owner approval queue.** When a tag will not scan (torn tag, broken scanner, dead camera) the salesman records the sale **by hand**: item name as they call it, optional size, quantity, sale price (no MRP cap: none known), credit to. It moves no stock and has no purchase price. **Every salesman entry (scanned or by hand, sale or exchange) waits for the owner**: one Approve / Reject per entry (reason chips), Approve all for a day or everything; only approved entries count in any total; the owner's own entries are approved as recorded | Owner: tags are on shirts and pants and sometimes cannot be read, but the sale must still be captured; he checks each entry himself | `approval_decisions` (append-only, one row per sale / exchange), `sales.manual` + nullable `product_id` / `mrp_paise`, movement reasons `sale_rejected` / `exchange_rejected` (migration 0007). Plan: `docs/PLAN_V12_HAND_ENTRY_APPROVALS.md` |
